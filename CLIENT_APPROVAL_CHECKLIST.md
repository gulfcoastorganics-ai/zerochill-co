# Client Approval Checklist

## Visual Identity

- Approve the obsidian / charcoal / crimson palette.
- Approve the terminal-first typography direction.
- Approve the tactical grid and CRT-inspired overlays.
- Approve the logo family and SVG asset system.

## Homepage Copy

- Approve the hero headline: `YOUR AI. UNPLUGGED.`
- Approve the supporting local-first / anti-cloud language.
- Approve the launch broadcast tone and cadence.

## Product Tier Names

- Sovereign Zero Lite
- Sovereign Zero Core
- Sovereign Zero Blacksite
- Zero-State Matrix DevKit

## Product Tier Claims

- Approve the positioning language for local inference.
- Approve the edge deployment framing.
- Approve the “concept” level hardware language versus any hard specifications.

## Preorder Language

- Approve the local-storage preorder fallback.
- Approve the field labels and success messaging.
- Approve the lead-capture tone and conversion path.

## Social Launch Copy

- Approve the banner copy and launch slogans.
- Approve the LinkedIn and X/Twitter treatment.
- Approve the GitHub social preview language.

## Docs Tone

- Approve the terminal-style documentation voice.
- Approve the technical preview blocks and operator framing.
- Approve the public/internal boundary for documentation.

## Deployment Ownership

- Approve who owns the production domain.
- Approve who owns future backend and preorder infrastructure.
- Approve whether the builder/operator continues deployment maintenance.

## Domain Decision

- Approve the final primary domain.
- Approve whether a staging domain remains public.
- Approve redirect and fallback behavior for direct route refreshes.
