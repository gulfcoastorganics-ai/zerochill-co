# ZeroChill Co Funding Continuation Proposal

## Executive Summary

ZeroChill Co has completed the zero-capital bootstrap phase. The public site is live, the product narrative is coherent, and the frontend now presents the platform as a believable sovereign infrastructure offering.

The next phase should be funded so the project can move from presentation-layer completion into operational continuation. That funding covers the missing pieces that investors, clients, and operators expect at this stage: domain ownership, database-backed records, email/domain verification, product assets, and continued development.

## What Has Been Built

- Public Vite + React site with a cinematic infrastructure aesthetic
- Routed product pages for:
  - Sovereign Zero Lite
  - Sovereign Zero Core
  - Sovereign Zero Blacksite
  - Zero-State Matrix DevKit
- Mission Control section with simulated telemetry and node state visuals
- Infrastructure topology, edge compute, and workstation schematics
- Client review and admin surfaces for preorder, export, deployment, and health status
- Product positioning, offline AI doctrine, and sovereign infrastructure storytelling

## Current Live Systems

- Deployed frontend site
- Product catalog and detail pages
- Review and admin routes
- Preorder entry points
- Static mission control and dashboard views
- Build pipeline on Vite

## Current Limitations

- No owned production domain workflow
- No database-backed storage for submissions or review records
- No verified email or domain identity layer
- Product asset set is still frontend-presentational, not fully productionized
- Continued development is constrained by the bootstrap-only budget model

## Why Funding Is Needed

ZeroChill has reached the end of the no-capital phase. The site can be seen, reviewed, and discussed, but the next phase requires paid infrastructure and time to make the platform feel complete and fundable.

The requested funding is specifically for:

- Domain registration and ownership
- Database setup for future intake and review records
- Email and domain verification
- Product asset creation and refinement
- Continued development work for the next delivery phase
